# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a small project, and the rule is simple: treat other people well.

Everyone taking part — asking a question, reporting a bug, sending a pull
request, reviewing one — should be able to do so without being harassed,
belittled or made unwelcome, whatever their background, experience level or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and be kind. Those are not in tension.
- Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith and knows things you do not.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no. It is a personal project with a
  deliberate scope, and "not for this project" is not a judgement of your idea.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit comments — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through the
[Security tab's private reporting](https://github.com/lilHammad99/warden/security/advisories/new),
which is the project's only confidential channel. Reports are handled
discreetly.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close contributions, or block an
account. For anything serious or repeated, expect a block without a warning.
